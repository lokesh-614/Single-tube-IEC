1. Psychrometrics & Wet-Bulb Wall Boundary Condition

The outer surface temperature is maintained at $T_{\text{wall}} = WBT_{\text{in}}$. Vapor pressure and wet-bulb temperature are computed using Tetens formulation and Carrier's psychrometric equation:

$$P_{\text{sat}}(T) = 611.21 \cdot \exp\left(\frac{17.502 \cdot T}{240.97 + T}\right) \quad [\text{Pa}]$$
$$\omega(T, RH, P) = 0.62198 \cdot \frac{RH \cdot P_{\text{sat}}(T)}{P - RH \cdot P_{\text{sat}}(T)} \quad [\text{kg}_w/\text{kg}_a]$$
$$\omega_{\text{in}} = \frac{(2501 - 2.3263 \cdot WBT)\omega_{\text{sat}}(WBT) - 1.006(T_{\text{in}} - WBT)}{2501 + 1.86 \cdot T_{\text{in}} - 4.186 \cdot WBT}$$

2. Internal Convection & Finite-Length Developing Flow Corrections

Nusselt numbers ($Nu$) incorporate thermal and hydrodynamic entry-length corrections for both laminar and turbulent regimes:

Laminar Regime ($Re < 2300$) — Hausen Entry-Length Correlation: $$Gz = \frac{D_i}{L} \cdot Re \cdot Pr, \qquad Nu = 3.66 + \frac{0.0668 \cdot Gz}{1 + 0.04 \cdot Gz^{2/3}}$$
Turbulent Regime ($Re \ge 2300$) — Gnielinski with Finite-Length Factor: $$Nu_{\text{fd}} = \frac{(f_{\text{fd}}/8)(Re - 1000)Pr}{1 + 12.7\sqrt{f_{\text{fd}}/8}\left(Pr^{2/3} - 1\right)}, \qquad C_{\text{entry}} = 1 + \left(\frac{D_i}{L}\right)^{0.7}$$ $$Nu = Nu_{\text{fd}} \cdot C_{\text{entry}}$$
Convective Heat & Mass Transfer Coefficients: $$h_i = \frac{Nu \cdot k_{\text{air}}}{D_i}, \qquad h_m = \frac{h_i}{\rho_{\text{air}} c_{p,\text{air}} Le^{2/3}} \quad (Le \approx 0.85)$$

3. Fluid Friction & Total Pressure Drop ($\Delta P$)

$$\text{Laminar: } f = \frac{64}{Re}\left(1 + 0.038 \cdot Gz^{0.75}\right), \qquad \text{Turbulent: } f = \left(0.790 \ln(Re) - 1.64\right)^{-2} \cdot \left[1 + \left(\frac{D_i}{L}\right)^{0.7}\right]$$
$$\Delta P = \sum_{j=1}^{N} f \cdot \left(\frac{dx}{D_i}\right) \cdot \frac{\rho_{\text{air}} v^2}{2}$$

4. 1D Axial Marching Heat & Mass Energy Balance

At each step $dx = L/N$, inner wall temperature $T_{s,i}$ is solved via energy balance across radial conduction resistance $R_{\text{cond}} = \frac{\ln(D_o/D_i)}{2\pi k dx}$:

$$Q_{\text{sens}} = h_i \, dA_i \, (T_{\text{air}} - T_{s,i}), \qquad Q_{\text{cond}} = \frac{T_{s,i} - T_{\text{wall}}}{R_{\text{cond}}}$$
$$\text{If } T_{s,i} < T_{\text{dp}}: \quad \dot{m}_{\text{cond}} = h_m \, dA_i \, \rho_{\text{air}} (\omega_{\text{air}} - \omega_{\text{sat}}(T_{s,i})), \qquad Q_{\text{lat}} = \dot{m}_{\text{cond}} \cdot h_{fg}$$
$$\frac{dT_{\text{air}}}{dx} = -\frac{Q_{\text{sens}}}{\dot{m}_a c_p}, \qquad \frac{d\omega_{\text{air}}}{dx} = -\frac{\dot{m}_{\text{cond}}}{\dot{m}_a}$$
